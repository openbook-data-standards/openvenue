# Agent rules — OpenVenue

OpenVenue is a public open-source standard. Treat it as frozen unless a human
has explicitly asked you to implement a named change.

## Do not invent anything

If a field name, object, enum, or behaviour was not asked and answered, do not
add it. Ask. Wait.

## Never change unless told

Do not edit spec, schemas, examples, or documents unless the human has clearly
said to implement that change. A question is not permission. "Should we…" is
not permission.

Default: **answer only**. No files, no commits, no PRs, no extras.

## After authorisation

Do the smallest change that matches the answer. Do not expand scope.
